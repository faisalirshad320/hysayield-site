Why longer CDs pay less right now

Normally a longer lock-up earns a higher rate. When longer terms pay less than shorter ones, the curve is "inverted", which usually means banks expect interest rates to fall. It makes short CDs and high-yield savings comparatively attractive, and long CDs worth it only if you find a rate well above the national average.
