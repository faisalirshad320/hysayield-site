Can you withdraw money from a high-yield savings account?

Yes, whenever you like. What to expect on timing, limits and fees.

Yes. You can take money out of a high-yield savings account at any time, and you keep the interest already earned. There is no early-withdrawal penalty — that applies to CDs, not savings accounts.

Withdrawals usually go by transfer to a linked checking account and arrive in one to three business days. Some banks still limit the number of withdrawals per month, even though the federal six-per-month rule was removed in 2020.

How to take money out

  1. Transfer to your linked checking account — the standard route. Initiate it in the app; money typically arrives in one to three business days, sometimes the next day.
  2. Wire transfer — same day at many banks, usually for a fee.
  3. ATM card or check — only if your bank offers one on the savings account. Most online HYSAs do not.

Plan for the delay: keep a month of spending in checking so an emergency does not wait on a transfer.

The six-withdrawals-a-month rule (Regulation D)

Until 2020, the Federal Reserve's Regulation D limited "convenient" transfers and withdrawals from savings accounts — online, phone, card and similar — to six a month. On April 24, 2020, the Federal Reserve Board removed that limit with an interim final rule (Federal Reserve announcement).

The rule change allows banks to drop the limit; it does not require them to. Some banks still cap savings withdrawals at six a month and charge an excess-transaction fee, or convert an account that is repeatedly over the limit to checking. Your account agreement says which applies.

Other limits to check

  • Daily or monthly transfer caps on how much you can move out electronically.
  • Minimum balance to keep earning the headline APY or to avoid a fee.
  • Holds on new deposits — money just transferred in may not be withdrawable for a few days.

What withdrawing does to your interest

Interest stops accruing on the amount you take out, and that is all. How long a balance lasts under regular withdrawals is what the withdrawal calculator works out.

Questions

Can you withdraw from a HYSA at any time?

Yes. There is no penalty, and you keep interest already earned. Some banks limit how many withdrawals you can make each month.

How many times can you withdraw from a high-yield savings account?

There is no federal limit since April 2020, when the Federal Reserve removed Regulation D's six-per-month cap. Your bank may still set its own limit; check the account agreement.

How long does it take to withdraw from a high-yield savings account?

Usually one to three business days by transfer to a linked checking account. A wire can be same-day, usually for a fee.

Do you lose interest if you withdraw from a HYSA?

No. Interest already credited is yours. You simply stop earning on the amount you withdraw.
